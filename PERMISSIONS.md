# Image permissions register

Track the reproduction permission for every third-party image before
each edition is distributed. Earlier editions' permissions may not
automatically cover a distributable EPUB/PDF — check.

## Cover

The cover is **typographic, with no photograph**, generated by
`build/make_cover.py`.

The Ninth Edition briefly carried a cover built from the photograph used
as the header of tenbratpress.org ("Java Gibraltar Fildes"). Stephen has
confirmed he does **not** hold reproduction permission for that image,
which is held by the National Library of New Zealand. It was removed
from the repository, the EPUB, and the download page on 2026-08-31.

Do not restore a photographic cover until a licensed image and its
credit line are recorded in the table below. Worth asking the National
Library of New Zealand directly — institutions commonly grant
non-commercial local-history use, and many pre-1945 New Zealand
photographs are out of copyright — but that is a question for them, not
an assumption for us.
