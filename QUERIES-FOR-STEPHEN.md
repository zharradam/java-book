# Queries for Stephen — first electronic edition

The text below was converted **verbatim** from tenbratpress.org — nothing
has been rewritten. During conversion the following came to light. None
have been "fixed" in the prose (except the purely mechanical items in
§B); each needs your decision.

## A. Editorial queries — your call

1. **Duplicated "Medical comforts" passage.** The Ronald Parsons
   paragraph about porter, arrowroot and laudanum appears twice: inside
   *"Java" leaves London* (the cleaner copy, reading "…\[William\] did
   not know…") and again as the standalone *Medical comforts?* section
   (this copy has the typos "laudanum laudenam" and
   "travellerstravelers"). Keep one? Suggest keeping the copy in
   *"Java" leaves London* and deleting the standalone section.

2. **Unresolved editorial note left in the text.** In *"Java" leaves
   London*, the paragraph about Trangmar boarding ends: "…arrived at
   Plymouth to take on the Cornish Emigrants. **Consistency needed here
   October 12th 1839, 18thof October**". Your note to yourself is still
   in the published text — which date stands?

3. **Possible missing text in the November 26th diary entry.** The entry
   ends: "…to avoid them and recover his course again." and then runs
   straight into "**would permit. 21° 5' South lat.** 32° 41' West
   long…" — the words before "would permit" appear to have been lost.
   Do you have the original to restore the gap?

4. **Introduction repeats itself.** The passenger/death figures
   ("approximately 500 passengers… 30 men, women and children
   perished… greatest loss among steerage… particularly the children")
   are stated twice in near-identical consecutive paragraphs. Merge?
   The second also contains "However, the The deaths did not stop…" —
   a doubled "the".

5. **Diary authorship wording.** The Preface settles on calling it "the
   Richard's Diary" since authorship can't be proven, but later chapters
   still attribute it directly to William in places, and the
   Bibliography entry notes it is "\[called incorrectly the George
   Richards diary\]". Happy for it to stand, or standardise the
   references?

6. **Masters and Owners table.** Converted from spaced text to a proper
   table. Three rows need you: **Duthie** appears alone with no year
   (master after Driver, for the 1839–40 emigrant voyage?); one **Somes
   London–Mediterranean** row has no year or master; the **1865
   Smith/Smith** row has no destination.

7. **Bibliography, Kerr entry.** "An Exelent Coliney The Practical
   Idealists of 1836–" — the range is cut off; the Preface gives it as
   1836–1846. Complete it?

8. **Mismatched bracket.** In *Migration to South Australia*: "…were
   <at the time) ascribed greater social importance…" — presumably
   "(at the time)"?

9. **Cover photo credit.** As a placeholder cover we used the photo from
   your site header ("Java Gibraltar Fildes"). Who should be credited,
   and does its permission cover use as the cover of the EPUB/PDF?

10. **Photos.** The site has no images; you mentioned having photos and
   other material. A list of what you have (and where each should sit in
   the text) is the next step — plus confirmation the old reproduction
   permissions cover a downloadable EPUB/PDF (see PERMISSIONS.md).
