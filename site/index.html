Some 413 emigrants were landed at Holdfast Bay. The book gathers their names from the manifest, the free-passage applications, the Register of Sick and Destitute Emigrants and descendants’ own records.

A melancholy voyage

The true story of the voyage of the East Indiaman Java, which carried emigrants to the colony of South Australia, arriving in Holdfast Bay on February 6th, 1840 — of the loss of life during the voyage, the subsequent enquiry, and the later history of the ship, sometimes known as the “Last of the East Indiamen”, to its final voyage to the breakers in Genoa in July 1939.
