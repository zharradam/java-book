# Product

## Platform

web

## Users
Primarily descendants of the *Java*'s 1839–40 emigrants: family historians, often older, arriving from a search or a family-history group, looking for an ancestor and for the story of the voyage their family made. Secondary: readers of South Australian, Cornish-emigration and maritime history.

## Product Purpose
theshipjava.uk is the free home of *Java: The Sailing Ship 1813–1940* by Stephen Michael Barnett (Ten Brat Press, ninth edition 2026). It lets visitors read the book online chapter by chapter, download it (EPUB, PDF, trial audiobook), and find the passenger and crew lists. Success: descendants find their ancestor and understand the voyage; the book is read and shared.

## Positioning
The only book-length history of this ship and voyage, built on passengers' diaries, the Medical Board enquiry, the Register of Sick and Destitute Emigrants and descendants' own records, published free.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Static site on GitHub Pages (custom domain via Cloudflare DNS); vanilla HTML/CSS/JS, no framework or server.
- Read-online pages under /read/ are generated from manuscript/*.md by Pandoc in CI; downloads are GitHub release assets.
- Must keep: SEO head (title, description, canonical, JSON-LD), download links, passenger/crew list links, audiobook note, obfuscated contact email.
- Interactive feature chosen for exploration: a voyage map/timeline (London → Plymouth → Holdfast Bay) with dated events and diary extracts from the book.

## Brand Commitments
- Title "Java", subtitle "The Sailing Ship 1813–1940", author Stephen Michael Barnett, publisher Ten Brat Press.
- The prose is never regenerated by software; quotations must be verbatim from manuscript/*.md.
- Images only from PERMISSIONS.md entries marked Confirmed, always with their credit line. No photographic cover; the National Library of New Zealand Gibraltar photo must not be used.
- Site wording and image choices are approved by the site maintainer (not Stephen).

## Evidence on Hand
manuscript/*.md (diaries, dates, lists), images/ with PERMISSIONS.md register. No testimonials, reviews or visitor figures exist; do not invent any.

## Product Principles
1. Ancestors first: a descendant should reach the passenger list or their family's story in one step.
2. The sources speak: let the diaries, posters and records carry the page; never invent history.
3. Readable by everyone: generous type, high contrast, works on an old phone and with a keyboard.
4. Free and plain: no tracking clutter, accounts or paywalls.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Older audience: large readable text, strong contrast, obvious links and buttons, motion that respects reduced-motion settings, full keyboard and screen-reader access to interactive content.
